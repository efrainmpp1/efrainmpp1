## 🔬 The Unified State Function (USF)

The **Unified State Function** is a quantum‑geometric framework that incorporates
degrees of freedom from Loop Quantum Gravity, string theory, and holography into
a single effective description. Instead of inventing new particles or fields,
the USF parametrises how quantum geometry affects low‑energy observables — both
in flavour physics and in cosmology.

Below are the two main tests performed so far.

---

## ⚛️ Flavour‑physics test: LHCb $P_5^\prime$ anomaly

- **First collider constraint on a quantum‑gravity effective theory**
- **Δχ² = 56.5** over the Standard Model → **7.5σ significance**
- No exotic mediators (leptoquarks, $Z^\prime$, SUSY) are required;
  the anomaly is explained by an **active quantum geometry coupling**

## 🌌 Cosmological test: the Hubble tension

- **Joint MCMC analysis** of DESI DR2 BAO, Pantheon+ SN Ia, and the SH0ES $H_0$ prior
- The USF model yields **$H_0 = 72.1 \pm 1.0$ km/s/Mpc**, fully consistent with
  local measurements, while $\Lambda$CDM is forced to $H_0 = 64.2 \pm 0.4$ km/s/Mpc
- **$\alpha_q = -0.100 \pm 0.012$** detected at **$>8\sigma$** → effective
  geometric screening at intermediate redshifts
- **$\Delta\mathrm{AIC} \approx -64.4$** in favour of the USF over $\Lambda$CDM

📄 **Preprint:** *Testing an effective geometric extension of $\Lambda$CDM: a joint MCMC analysis of DESI, Pantheon+, and SH0ES data*

🔗 [Repository: usf-desi-cosmology](https://github.com/PantaleonSystems/usf-desi-cosmology) | [Zenodo DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20263285)
